Abstract—In recent years, as the Power Internet of Things 
continues to advance and improve, the routing requires 
efficient and reliable optimization methods to ensure stable 
operation. However, sensor nodes in Wireless Sensor Networks 
for Power Internet of Things have limited energy and lifespan, 
making the search for an efficient routing optimization method 
a critical issue. This paper innovatively proposes a novel high-
reliable Wireless Sensor Networks routing optimization 
method for Power Internet of Things—the Multi-Objective 
Chaotic Seagull Optimization Algorithm (MCSOA). MCSOA 
integrates chaotic optimization strategies with the Seagull 
Optimization Algorithm, enable to escape local optima and 
improving its global search capability. Additionally, it 
incorporates multi-objective functions to address various key 
factors in PSWSN routing, including minimizing transmission 
loss, balancing load distribution, and enhancing system 
reliability. To evaluate the effectiveness of the optimization 
method, extensive new experiments were conducted, accurately 
reflecting real-world power grid operation scenarios. The 
experimental findings indicate that MCSOA significantly 
outperforms traditional algorithms like Genetic Algorithm and 
Particle 
Swarm 
Optimization 
in 
terms 
of 
efficiency. 
Specifically, MCSOA cuts down energy use by a minimum of 
16.1% and prolongs the lifespan of the PSWSN by at least 
15.6%. 
Keywords—Wireless Sensor Networks, Power Internet of 
Things, Routing optimization, Seagull Optimization algorithm 
I. 
 INTRODUCTION 
Wireless sensor networks are crucial in the ongoing 
advancement of the Power Internet of Things. In PIoT, 
WSNs are utilized for monitoring and managing power 
distribution, ensuring efficient energy consumption, and 
preventing system failures. Recently, wireless sensor 
networks in power systems (PSWSNs) have garnered 
increasing research interest and attention [1][2]. 
A key challenge in designing PSWSNs is optimizing the 
routing process to ensure networks’ high reliability and 
efficiency[3]. Given that sensor nodes are typically equipped 
with limited power and lifespan, the routing optimization 
significantly impacts the overall performance of the network. 
So, it’s essential to enhance the routing efficiency to 
maximize the network's reliability and lifespan. In PSWSNs, 
routing optimization is crucial for enhancing network 
performance [4][5]. But, determining the best routing paths 
is an NP-hard problem, which complicates achieving the 
optimal solution due to the high computational complexity of 
exhaustive search methods is impractical for real-world 
applications. Traditional deterministic algorithms often fail 
to provide effective routing optimization methods in large-
scale PSWSNs. With the deepening research on swarm 
intelligence heuristic algorithms, various optimization 
algorithms have emerged, such as the Genetic Algorithm[6] 
and Particle Swarm Optimization[7]. However, these routing 
optimization algorithms, which simulate the intelligent 
behavior of biological populations, often fall into local 
optima due to premature convergence when obtaining the 
optimal routing scheme. This situation can increase network 
energy consumption and shorten network lifetime. 
Therefore, this paper innovatively proposes a new high-
reliability wireless sensor network routing optimization 
method for the Power Internet of Things, namely the Multi-
Objective 
Chaotic 
Seagull 
Optimization 
Algorithm 
(MCSOA), which significantly extends network lifetime and 
reduces node energy consumption. Additionally, the 
introduction of chaotic optimization strategies and seagull 
optimization algorithms enhances the algorithm's ability to 
escape local optima, improving global search capabilities. 
To achieve these goals, this research makes the following 
contributions: 
• Firstly, it proposes MCSOA for solving the routing 
optimization problem in WSNs for the PIoT. M-

III. 
MCSOA SOLVES THE TARFET ALLOCATION 
PROBLEM IN PSMSNS 
In this section, we introduce the MCSOA to solve the 
task allocation problem in PSMSNs. The MCSOA leverages 
the advantages of chaos theory and quantum computing to 
enhance the optimization performance of the traditional 
SOA. 
The Multi-Objective Chaotic Seagull Optimization 
Algorithm (MCSOA) is an advanced optimization technique 
inspired by the natural behaviors of seagulls, particularly 
their migration and attacking patterns. This algorithm 
integrates chaotic maps and multi-objective optimization 
principles to enhance the search capabilities and convergence 
properties of the traditional Seagull Optimization Algorithm 
(SOA). The MCSOA is designed to solve complex 
optimization problems by maintaining a balance between 
exploration and exploitation, ensuring a diverse and 
convergent set of solutions. 
Seagulls exhibit distinct behaviors during their migration 
and 
hunting 
processes. 
These 
behaviors 
can 
be 
mathematically modeled to create effective optimization 
strategies: 
Migration: Seagulls migrate in groups towards regions 
with abundant resources. This behavior is modeled to 
represent the exploration phase of the algorithm. 
Attacking: During hunting, seagulls attack their prey 
using spiral flight patterns. This behavior is used to simulate 
the exploitation phase, focusing on refining solutions to 
achieve optimal results.

IV. 
SIMULATION AND RESAULT ANALYSIS 
This section outlines the simulation setup and results 
used to assess MCSOA's effectiveness in optimizing routing 
for PSWSNs. We compared MCSOA with traditional GA 
and PSO methods using simulations on a computer equipped 
with an Intel(R) Core(TM) i7-10700K CPU @ 3.80GHz. 
For our experiments, we considered various scenarios 
with different numbers of nodes (50, 100, 150, and 200). 
Each algorithm was executed for 300 iterations in each 
scenario. Key metrics evaluated included node lifespan, 
energy consumption, and data packets received by the base 
station (BS).

V. 
CONCLUSIONS 
In this paper, we proposed the MCSOA for optimizing 
routing in high-reliability PSWSNs context. The MCSOA 
integrates chaotic maps with seagull optimization, enhancing 
the algorithm's capability to prevent getting trapped in local 
optima and strike a balance between exploration and 
exploitation. 
Through extensive simulations and comparative analysis, 
MCSOA demonstrated superior performance in terms of 
convergence speed, solution diversity, and reliability when 
compared to existing algorithms. The chaotic maps 
incorporated into MCSOA significantly improved the search 
efficiency and robustness, making it a promising solution for 
the complex nature of WSNs in power systems. 
Moreover, the dynamic archive mechanism employed by 
MCSOA ensures the retention of Pareto optimal solutions, 
further contributing to the algorithm's effectiveness in multi-
objective optimization scenarios. The empirical results 
validated the algorithm's applicability and efficiency in real-
world PIoT environments. 
In summary, MCSOA provides a novel and effective 
approach to routing optimization in PIoT, offering a high-
reliability solution that meets the stringent requirements of 
modern power systems. Future work will focus on further 
refining the algorithm, exploring additional chaotic maps, 
and expanding its application to other optimization problems 
in the field of cybersecurity and beyond.
