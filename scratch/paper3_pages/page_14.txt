CENSORSHIP RESISTANCE PROTOCOL
One issue existing in prior work [32], [34] that OmniLedger
partially addresses is when a malicious shard leader censors
transactions. This attack can be undetectable from the rest
of the shard’s validators. A leader who does not propose a
transaction is acceptable as far as the state is concerned, but
this attack can compromise the fairness of the system or be
used as a coercion tool.

are censored. They can either collect those transactions via the
normal gossiping process or receive a request directly from a
